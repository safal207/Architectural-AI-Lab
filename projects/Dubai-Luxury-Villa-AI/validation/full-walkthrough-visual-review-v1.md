# Full Walkthrough Visual Review v1

## Purpose

The focused beads have now stabilized the Upper Landing geometry, Master Bedroom framing, Day / Evening / Night hierarchy and textured walnut colour factor.

The next step is to stop judging rooms in isolation and inspect the complete authored client journey in one consistent Day presentation.

## Evidence route

The browser capture visits the real bounded first-person stops in order:

1. Main entry
2. Living room
3. Kitchen + dining
4. Stair hall
5. Upper landing
6. Master bedroom
7. Pool terrace

Every frame uses:

- Day lighting;
- Warm Limestone finish mood;
- source GLB geometry/material maps;
- `family-microcontrast-v1` response;
- the bounded walk graph;
- runtime-only lighting.

## Why Day is the baseline

Day is used as the neutral design-review baseline because it exposes geometry, material separation and spatial composition without relying on dramatic night lighting to hide weak areas.

Evening/Night are already separately covered at the Master Bedroom and can be expanded later for final portfolio showcase frames.

## Automated contract

For every authored stop:

- model state must be `loaded`;
- view mode must be first-person;
- walk graph must be `ready`;
- material response must be `family-microcontrast-v1`;
- all four material families must be recognized;
- light engine must be `runtime-only`;
- expected local lighting profile must be active;
- browser console/page errors must be zero.

The workflow uploads seven fixed-view screenshots plus `report.json` as `dubai-villa-full-walkthrough-day`.

## Visual review rubric

For every frame review the same five questions:

1. **Geometry** — is the space physically believable and unobstructed?
2. **Material** — do stone, plaster, timber and other key surfaces remain readable?
3. **Light** — is the space legible without blown highlights or dead black zones?
4. **Camera / depth** — is there foreground -> midground -> destination structure?
5. **Proof** — does the browser frame look strong enough that a freelance client would understand the property without explanation?

## Prioritization rule

Do not polish all seven frames at once.

After evidence is captured, identify the single weakest client-facing frame and repair the smallest justified layer only. Repeat until no frame contains a portfolio-blocking defect.

Expected remaining risk from previous evidence: Living Room local light hierarchy / dark right-side massing. This is a hypothesis, not a pre-decided fix; the seven-frame evidence decides the next bead.
