# Dubai Luxury Villa AI — v0.3-r1.1 Visual Critique

Status: **FORM GATE — PARTIAL PASS / ONE TARGET MISSED**

Review basis: direct A/B between `renders/villa-v0.3-r1-clay.png` and `renders/villa-v0.3-r1.1-clay.png`. The workflow enforces the identical hero camera: location `(24.8, 28.6, 1.72)`, 40 mm lens, `shift_y=0.075`.

## What improved versus r1

- The roof/cantilever reads thinner and less dominant.
- The upper glazing is visibly more recessed, with a clearer frame/depth hierarchy.
- Pool and terrace edge stacking is cleaner and less procedural.
- Foreground layer edges are physically thinner; the model-base effect is reduced, although the broad ground surface still occupies substantial foreground.
- The composition remains stable because the A/B camera contract was held constant.

## Important miss discovered in the A/B

The intended **screen-left blank ground core** was not the world-space object assumed during r1.1 authoring. The new recess landed on the opposite lower core in screen space. This is visible in the candidate as a new narrow dark recess on the screen-right side while the large screen-left core remains mostly blank.

This is a useful pipeline finding: visual critique targets must be mapped from screen-space evidence to the actual scene object before geometry edits. World-axis naming alone is not a safe proxy for what the hero camera shows.

## Remaining blocker

- Break/recess the actual screen-left ground core (`ground_right_private_core` in world-space) with one controlled shadow/recess gesture and slightly reduce its mass.
- Remove the unintended opposite-side r1.1 recess so the lower composition stays quiet.

## Gate decision

Do **not** start final materials yet.

Next step: **v0.3-r1.2 surgical form correction**, same clay lighting and exactly the same A/B camera. No additional style changes until this screen-space massing issue is resolved.

The project remains an architectural visualization prototype, not BIM or construction documentation.
