# Dubai Luxury Villa AI — v0.3 M2 Material Critique

Status: **MATERIAL GATE — PARTIAL PASS / ONE MORE CONTROLLED ITERATION**

Review basis: actual native Blender output `renders/villa-v0.3-m2-material.png`, compared against M1 using the same frozen v0.3-r1.2 geometry, 40 mm hero camera and neutral review lighting.

## What improved vs M1

- Glazing is lighter and no longer reads as uniformly black.
- Pool water has a clearer aqua body and a more readable surface.
- Timber midtones are slightly more legible while remaining restrained.
- The material system remains coherent and does not introduce visual clutter.
- Form integrity stayed frozen; no geometry was changed to make the materials look better.

## What still blocks the Material gate

1. **Primary limestone still does not separate strongly enough from plaster.** At hero distance the villa remains too close to a monochrome white/grey object.
2. **The intended stone identity is not tactile enough.** The surface relief is technically present, but the primary stone needs a subtle, architectural travertine-like tonal structure that survives at building scale.
3. **Glass still needs a more elegant smoke/reflection balance.** It is improved, but some bays remain too dark compared with the premium hospitality reference direction.
4. **Pool colour is improved but still too washed out in the full composition.** It needs a slightly deeper body while keeping a calm, non-cyan look.
5. **Review exposure is slightly too bright for judging the material value hierarchy.** M3 should lower neutral exposure modestly, not switch to cinematic lighting.

## M3 targets

- Use a controlled warm limestone/travertine material with subtle large-scale horizontal tonal variation, not noisy diffuse wallpaper.
- Keep plaster genuinely quiet ivory and clearly lighter than the primary stone.
- Lower neutral review exposure slightly so stone/plaster/deck hierarchy can be judged honestly.
- Make glass a refined smoke-blue grey with controlled reflectance and enough transmission to avoid black-panel appearance.
- Deepen the pool to a muted mineral aqua.
- Keep timber, geometry, camera, entourage visibility and composition unchanged.

## Gate decision

Do **not** enter the cinematic Light stage yet.

Proceed to **v0.3 M3 final material-gate study**. If the primary stone/plaster/glass/water hierarchy reads clearly from the locked hero camera without dramatic lighting, freeze materials and move to Light.
