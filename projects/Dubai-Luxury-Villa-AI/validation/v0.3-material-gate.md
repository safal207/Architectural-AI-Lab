# Dubai Luxury Villa AI — v0.3 Material Gate

Stage order: **Form -> Material -> Light -> Life -> Presentation**

Form baseline is frozen at `v0.3-r1.2` after the clay FORM gate passed. Material iterations must not alter the architectural massing simply to improve a render.

## M1 target language

**Quiet luxury / resort hospitality / modern desert elegance.**

The exterior palette is intentionally restrained:

- warm limestone / travertine-like primary stone;
- quiet mineral plaster for secondary masses;
- walnut/teak-toned timber only on controlled accent planes;
- graphite metal for frames, shadow datums and slim structural details;
- neutral architectural glass with readable depth rather than mirror-black glazing;
- calm low-roughness pool water with subtle micro-ripple;
- matte warm deck stone.

## Acceptance criteria

A material study can pass only if all of the following are visually true from the locked hero camera:

1. The primary stone reads as a premium mineral surface, not noisy wallpaper or polished plastic.
2. Plaster is visibly quieter than stone and does not compete with the main facade anchor.
3. Timber is warm and selective; it must not turn the villa orange or rustic.
4. Graphite details remain slim and subordinate to the architecture.
5. Glass has believable reflection/transmission balance and preserves facade depth.
6. Water reflects the building and sky without becoming a bright cyan slab.
7. Texture scale is believable at building scale; no obvious tiling dominates the hero image.
8. Roughness hierarchy remains visible: plaster/stone matte, timber satin-matte, metal controlled, glass/water smoother.
9. The palette remains coherent when lifestyle furniture and vegetation are hidden.
10. The material pass improves perceived value without relying on dramatic sunset/bloom/post effects.

## Review protocol

- Use the exact `v0.3-r1.2` geometry and locked 40 mm hero camera.
- Render at 1600x900.
- Use neutral late-afternoon review lighting.
- Hide lifestyle furniture, decorative planting and entourage.
- Preserve a validation receipt with SHA-256 for PNG, GLB and BLEND outputs.
- Mark the result `PASS`, `PARTIAL PASS`, or `FAIL` only after visual inspection of the actual Blender render.

## Claim boundary

This remains an AI-assisted architectural visualization prototype. It is not BIM, construction documentation, engineering analysis, code-compliance evidence, valuation, or a sales forecast.
