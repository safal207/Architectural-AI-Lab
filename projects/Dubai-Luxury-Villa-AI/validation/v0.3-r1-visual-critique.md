# Dubai Luxury Villa AI — v0.3-r1 Visual Critique

Status: **FORM GATE — PARTIAL PASS / ITERATE BEFORE MATERIALS**

Review basis: generated `renders/villa-v0.3-r1-clay.png` from the native Blender v0.3-r1 workflow.

## What improved

- The pool, terrace, and living facade now read as one stronger horizontal composition.
- The upper volume is more intentionally asymmetric than v0.2-r9.
- The lowered human-height camera gives the building more believable architectural presence.
- Removing vegetation, furniture, and lifestyle clutter makes the massing easier to judge.
- The stone spine / lighter upper volume relationship is clearer.

## What still blocks the Form gate

1. **Roof hierarchy is too dominant.** The large upper roof/cantilever currently reads as a very wide floating plate and pulls too much visual weight upward.
2. **Left ground core is still too blank and heavy.** It reads as a large closed box against the otherwise open pavilion.
3. **Upper facade remains too box-like.** The composition is cleaner, but it still needs more depth/recess hierarchy to feel genuinely high-end rather than procedural.
4. **Site/plinth foreground is visually heavy.** The thick foreground bands compete with the villa and make the building feel like a scale model on a base.
5. **Pool is coherent but still reads slightly as an inserted rectangle.** It needs a stronger edge/terrace integration before the final material pass.

## r1.1 geometry targets

- Reduce the roof/cantilever visual thickness and trim its lateral overhang.
- Break or recess the blank left ground core with one controlled shadow reveal or entry datum — no decorative patterning.
- Increase depth hierarchy at the upper facade: glass plane recessed behind a thinner stone/timber frame.
- Reduce the visible foreground plinth thickness from the hero camera.
- Integrate the pool edge into the terrace with one cleaner datum and less stacked geometry.

## Gate decision

Do **not** start the final material pass yet.

Next step: **v0.3-r1.1 Form refinement**, then render the same neutral clay camera for direct A/B comparison.

The project remains an architectural visualization prototype, not BIM or construction documentation.
