# Dubai Luxury Villa AI — v0.3 M3 Material Critique

Status: **MATERIAL GATE — PARTIAL PASS / REMOVE PROCEDURAL BAND ARTIFACT**

Review basis: actual native Blender render `renders/villa-v0.3-m3-material.png`, same frozen v0.3-r1.2 form and locked 40 mm camera.

## What improved

- The primary stone is finally visually distinct from the quieter plaster.
- Warm/cool hierarchy is clearer and the villa no longer reads as a uniformly white/grey model.
- Glass is more refined and readable than M1/M2.
- Timber, graphite and stone now work as a coherent quiet-luxury palette.
- Pool and deck hierarchy is directionally stronger.

## What blocks final Material PASS

The travertine tonal structure is **too visibly banded** on the upper/right stone masses. From the hero camera the repeated horizontal waves read as procedural decoration rather than natural stone. This violates the material-gate requirement that texture scale and variation must not dominate the architecture.

This is not a geometry problem and should not trigger another form iteration.

## M4 surgical target

- Keep the M3 palette and all non-stone materials unchanged.
- Replace the visible wave-band colour pattern with low-amplitude organic mineral variation using close warm tones.
- Preserve real micro-normal relief but avoid readable repeated stripes.
- Slightly deepen pool body colour if needed, without turning it cyan.
- Keep geometry, camera, neutral review lighting and lifestyle visibility unchanged.

## Gate decision

Proceed to **v0.3 M4 surgical stone cleanup**. If the repeated banding disappears while the warm stone/plaster hierarchy remains, freeze the material system and enter the Light stage.

Claim boundary: architectural visualization prototype only; not BIM or construction documentation.
