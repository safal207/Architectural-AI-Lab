## What L1 improved

- The villa finally reads as a time-of-day image rather than a neutral material test.
- The low sun gives the cantilevers, roof planes and facade fins stronger depth.
- Timber/graphite edges pick up restrained warm highlights instead of disappearing into the neutral study light.
- The pool catches a useful specular line and begins to participate in the hero composition.
- Warm interior cues are present behind the main glazing without introducing lifestyle geometry.
- The lighting change did not alter the locked form, camera or M4 material baseline.

## What blocks a Light-gate PASS

1. **The horizon is too amber and too flat.** The warm band reads more like a CG backdrop than a premium Dubai photographic sky.
2. **The right/private volume falls too dark.** Its mineral surface loses too much texture and the material hierarchy collapses in shadow.
3. **Ground-floor glazing has narrow hot vertical highlights.** They read as light-card/reflection artefacts rather than broad, believable interior warmth.
4. **The foreground/site plinth is under-shaped.** It becomes a dark slab and competes with the villa instead of quietly grounding it.
5. **Warm/cool separation is not yet balanced.** The architecture needs more cool sky recovery on the shadow side while keeping the sun directional.
6. **Pool colour is partially lost under the warm cast.** The M4 muted mineral-aqua should remain legible at hero exposure.

## L2 correction — lighting only

- lift the sun slightly and reduce its orange bias;
- increase broad cool sky fill;
- add a restrained right-side recovery light for the private volume;
- make camera-side bounce slightly stronger but more neutral;
- reduce interior-light saturation while keeping a warm hospitality cue;
- calm the Nishita horizon by raising solar elevation and reducing dust;
- preserve the exact FORM, camera, M4 materials and hidden Life-stage geometry.
