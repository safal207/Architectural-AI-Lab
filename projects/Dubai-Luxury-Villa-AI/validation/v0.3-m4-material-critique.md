# Dubai Luxury Villa AI — v0.3 M4 Material Critique

Status: **MATERIAL GATE — PASS / FREEZE MATERIAL BASELINE**

Review basis: actual native Blender output `renders/villa-v0.3-m4-material.png`, inspected after M1 -> M2 -> M3 -> M4 iterations using the same frozen `v0.3-r1.2` form and locked 40 mm hero camera.

## Why M4 passes

- **Primary stone is distinct but restrained.** The warm mineral mass now separates from the quiet plaster without becoming decorative.
- **M3 procedural banding is gone.** The stone variation is organic and low-amplitude rather than visibly repeated horizontal stripes.
- **Plaster remains subordinate.** It reads as the calm secondary surface rather than competing with the stone anchor.
- **Timber stays selective and warm.** It adds hospitality character at the soffit/fins without turning the facade orange or rustic.
- **Graphite details stay thin and architectural.** Frames and shadow datums support the massing rather than dominate it.
- **Glass reads as controlled smoke-blue architectural glazing.** It is no longer a uniform black panel system.
- **Pool water has a muted mineral-aqua body.** It remains calm and reflective instead of becoming a saturated cyan feature.
- **Deck/ground hierarchy is clearer.** Hardscape is visually separate from the facade while remaining neutral.
- **The palette works under neutral review lighting.** The result does not require sunset, bloom or post-processing to create material hierarchy.
- **Geometry and camera were not changed.** Material improvement did not cheat the previously passed FORM baseline.

## Frozen v0.3 material language

1. Organic warm limestone / travertine-like primary stone
2. Quiet ivory mineral plaster
3. Warm walnut/teak-toned timber
4. Graphite architectural metal
5. Smoke-blue neutral architectural glass
6. Muted mineral-aqua pool water
7. Matte warm deck stone

## Remaining work is not a Material-gate blocker

The current review intentionally hides lifestyle furniture, vegetation and cinematic lighting. Some spaces therefore still feel sparse or model-like. Those concerns belong to the next stages:

- **Light:** golden-hour / blue-hour photographic hierarchy, warm interior life and controlled reflections.
- **Life:** curated desert landscape, restrained furniture/entourage and hospitality cues.
- **Presentation:** final hero angles, viewer integration and portfolio delivery.
