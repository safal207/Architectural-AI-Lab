# Dubai Luxury Villa v0.2 — Visual Critique 02

Status: **FAIL — improving, still not portfolio hero**

Iteration: r2
Source render: `renders/villa-v0.2-hero.png`

## Confirmed improvements

- The previous debug-like circular terrace light pools are gone.
- Camera is lower and facade/pool hierarchy is clearer.
- Upper glazing now has readable interior depth.
- The pool surface is lighter and participates in the composition.
- Facade seams, soffit treatment and dark edge trims add scale cues.
- Landscape geometry is smoother than the first render.

## Remaining highest-impact defects

### R2-W1 — Sky is too orange and synthetic

The Nishita setup produces a strong orange field that reads like CG backdrop rather than restrained Dubai blue hour.

**Work order:** switch to a controlled blue-hour gradient with a subtle warm horizon, not an orange full-frame sky.

### R2-W2 — Camera is still slightly model-like

The roof and site are still shown from a somewhat elevated overview angle.

**Work order:** lower camera again, tighten to a 50–55 mm architectural-photo feel, keep verticals calm, and prioritize living facade + pool.

### R2-W3 — Material realism remains insufficient

Stone/plaster still read as procedural solids more than tactile natural surfaces.

**Work order:** move hero render to Cycles CPU, keep micro-bump subtle, deepen stone tonal variation and introduce softer physically based shadows/reflections.

### R2-W4 — Landscape remains a realism tell

Large standalone tree silhouettes are too synthetic and pull attention away from the architecture.

**Work order:** use restrained layered planting, one procedural palm with tapered/curved fronds, and lower shrubs instead of spherical crowns.

### R2-W5 — Pool needs physical depth

The water reads better but still looks like a thin surface laid on a model.

**Work order:** create a readable pool floor/depth layer, refine coping and let cooler sky reflections carry the water.

### R2-W6 — Architectural lighting needs visible logic

The scene is calmer, but lighting should appear to originate from concealed strips/soffits rather than invisible scene lights.

**Work order:** add narrow emissive architectural strips and use low-energy area lights only to support them.
