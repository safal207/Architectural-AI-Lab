## Why the form gate now passes

- The actual screen-left lower core is now reduced in mass and broken by one controlled vertical recess. It no longer reads as a single blank procedural box.

- The roof/cantilever hierarchy remains substantially thinner and calmer than r1.

- The pool/terrace composition remains cleaner, with fewer stacked edge bands.
- Foreground base edges are materially thinner than r1. The broad ground plane still occupies foreground in the locked hero camera, but this is no longer a massing blocker.

- Do not add more massing gestures. The next stage should improve perceived quality through material scale, glazing, water, lighting, and restrained landscape rather than adding geometry for its own sake.
