# Dubai Luxury Villa v0.2 — Visual Critique 05

## Reviewed artifact

`renders/villa-v0.2-hero.png` from critic iteration r5 (Eevee + CC0 HDRI/PBR).

## Verdict

**WOW Gate: FAIL, near the first credible portfolio-candidate zone.**

The environment and material response now feel substantially less synthetic. The remaining visual gap is dominated by architectural detailing, planting language and lifestyle cues.

## Five highest-impact visible defects

1. **The villa is still too block-like at close inspection.** The massing is coherent, but facade edges need more designed layers, shadow gaps and balcony detail.
2. **Planting remains visibly procedural.** Rounded masses are cleaner than earlier trees, but they still read as CG placeholders.
3. **The pool is controlled but under-sold.** It needs a richer teal reflection and subtle underwater illumination to become a luxury focal element.
4. **The glazing is dark with limited lifestyle depth.** Interior curtains, warm linear detail and a few readable furnishings should make the residence feel occupied.
5. **The hero frame still feels like a polished model render rather than a finished architectural photograph.** Camera and foreground detail need one more composed pass.

## Work orders for r6

- Add a slim frameless upper balcony railing and complete the master facade frame.
- Add subtle facade shadow gaps / panel scale cues without clutter.
- Add warm interior curtains and restrained pendant/linear light detail behind glazing.
- Add underwater pool accent lights and rebalance water material.
- Remove rounded shrub clusters and replace them with procedural agave / ornamental grass planting suited to a dry luxury landscape.
- Pull camera slightly closer while preserving the full roof silhouette.
- Do not add UI or additional rooms until the exterior hero either passes or reaches a clear hard limit for the current procedural approach.
