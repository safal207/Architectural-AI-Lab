# Dubai Luxury Villa v0.2 — Visual Critique 01

Status: **FAIL — do not ship**

Source render: `renders/villa-v0.2-hero.png`

This critique is intentionally separate from technical CI. The render, GLB and Blender source can all be valid while the visual target still fails.

## What improved versus v0.1

- The building now reads as a two-storey contemporary residence rather than a stack of anonymous blocks.
- A deep roof/cantilever creates a recognisable horizontal silhouette.
- The pool is part of the main composition.
- Warm interior light creates some indoor/outdoor connection.
- The living-room glazing and terrace relationship are clearer.

## Highest-impact visual defects

### W1 — Camera still feels like a model overview

The current camera is too elevated and shows too much roof/site geometry. It reads closer to a design-model overview than a premium architectural photograph.

**Work order:** lower the camera to a near-human elevated eye level, keep a 45–55 mm lens, and move the focal hierarchy toward facade + living room + pool reflection.

### W2 — Environment is a black void

The dark background has no believable blue-hour sky or horizon. This makes the scene feel synthetic and removes atmosphere.

**Work order:** replace the flat dark world with a physically plausible blue-hour sky treatment and a restrained horizon/ground context.

### W3 — Exterior light pools are visibly artificial

Large circular pools of light on the terrace read as debug lights rather than architecture lighting.

**Work order:** remove or drastically reduce the exposed point lights. Use softer grazing/soffit light with believable fixtures and falloff.

### W4 — Glass reads too dark

Upper glazing becomes nearly black and loses interior depth. The facade needs readable glazing, subtle reflections, mullions and warm depth behind the glass.

**Work order:** brighten/refine glass transmission and give the upper floor an interior layer behind glazing.

### W5 — Vegetation looks low-poly

The two trees read as obvious faceted game assets and immediately break the luxury-archviz illusion.

**Work order:** replace them with smoother procedural canopies / palms / layered planting silhouettes, and smooth shading where appropriate.

### W6 — Material hierarchy is still weak

Stone, plaster and roof surfaces do not yet read as premium natural materials. The large roof plane is especially flat and visually dominant.

**Work order:** refine travertine contrast and scale, add stone joints/reveals, timber soffit detail and darker edge trims; avoid large undifferentiated slabs.

### W7 — Pool is dark and visually secondary

The water should be one of the hero image anchors, but it currently reads as a dark slot.

**Work order:** lift water readability, improve edge detail and reflection contribution, and let the pool visually lead the eye toward the living space.
