# Dubai Luxury Villa v0.2 — Visual Critique 06

## Reviewed artifact

`renders/villa-v0.2-hero.png` from critic iteration r6.

## Verdict

**WOW Gate: FAIL, current visual level ~7/10.**

The residence now reads as a coherent luxury-villa concept rather than a primitive blockout. Balcony glass, facade joints, PBR surfaces, agave planting and interior detail all improved the frame. The remaining defects are local and presentation-specific.

## Five highest-impact visible defects

1. **Three bright circular pool-light patches are an immediate CG tell.** They must be removed.
2. **The visible mountainous HDRI conflicts with the Dubai positioning.** Keep HDRI lighting/reflections, but present a clean camera-visible blue-hour sky.
3. **The left roof/cantilever is clipped by framing.** Preserve the complete hero silhouette.
4. **Ornamental grass still reads as cylinders/sticks.** Replace with thin curved ribbon blades.
5. **The composition still has too much dark foreground and insufficient photographic breathing room around the architecture.** Rebalance the camera without making the villa small.

## Work orders for r7

- Use a Light Path world split: HDRI for non-camera rays; Nishita/clean sky for camera rays.
- Remove point-like underwater pool lights; let water read through reflection, color and one broad soft fill.
- Replace cylinder grass with thin curved mesh ribbons.
- Pull camera back just enough to preserve the full roof and pool silhouette.
- Reduce dark foreground dominance and keep the residence centered as the hero subject.
- Do not add more rooms or UI.
