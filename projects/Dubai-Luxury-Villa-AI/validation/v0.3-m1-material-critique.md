# Dubai Luxury Villa AI — v0.3 M1 Material Critique

Status: **MATERIAL GATE — PARTIAL PASS / ITERATE**

Review basis: native Blender output `renders/villa-v0.3-m1-material.png` generated from the frozen `v0.3-r1.2` FORM baseline.

## What works

- The restrained palette is directionally correct: no glossy marble overload, no neon, no decorative material noise.
- Timber soffit gives the roof a warmer hospitality cue without changing the massing.
- Graphite frames create crisp architectural hierarchy.
- The neutral review lighting exposes the materials instead of hiding them behind a sunset look.
- The frozen v0.3-r1.2 form still reads cleanly under real materials.

## What blocks the Material gate

1. **Stone and plaster are too close in value and temperature.** From the hero camera the villa reads mostly cool grey/white, so the intended warm limestone anchor is not legible enough.
2. **Glass is too black.** The glazing reads as dark panels rather than architectural glass with reflection/transmission depth.
3. **Pool water is too pale/transparent from this angle.** It loses the intended calm aqua depth and reads closer to a reflective white surface.
4. **Deck/site hierarchy is weak.** Foreground hardscape is too similar in value to the building and keeps some of the scale-model feeling.
5. **Timber is promising but too dark on some vertical elements.** It should remain warm walnut/teak, not drift into near-black brown.

## M2 targets

- Warm the primary limestone and separate it clearly from the quieter ivory plaster.
- Increase glazing transmission/readability while keeping reflections controlled.
- Give the pool a deeper desaturated aqua body and slightly stronger micro-ripple.
- Darken/warm the deck enough to separate ground plane from facade without adding visual noise.
- Lift timber midtones while preserving a satin-matte finish.
- Keep geometry, camera and lifestyle visibility unchanged.

## Gate decision

Do **not** start the cinematic Light stage yet.

Proceed to **v0.3 M2 material refinement** using the same frozen form and locked hero camera.

Claim boundary: architectural visualization prototype only; not BIM or construction documentation.
