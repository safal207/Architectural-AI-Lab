# Dubai Luxury Villa v0.2 — Visual Critique 04

## Reviewed artifact

`renders/villa-v0.2-hero.png` from critic iteration r4 (Eevee archviz pass).

## Verdict

**WOW Gate: FAIL, but materially improved.**

The frame is now clean and readable. Architecture, glazing and facade hierarchy are substantially stronger than r1–r3. The remaining gap is mostly visual fidelity rather than pipeline correctness.

## Five highest-impact visible defects

1. **Materials still read as procedural / synthetic.** Stone, plaster and timber lack photographic micro-detail and believable variation.
2. **Landscape is the strongest CG tell.** The palm and rounded shrub masses look generated rather than planted.
3. **The sky is too pale and generic.** It creates a clean frame but not a convincing high-end exterior atmosphere.
4. **Pool surface is over-bright and under-detailed.** It does not yet provide the reflective premium-water effect expected in luxury archviz.
5. **The framing clips too much architecture on the left and still feels like a model showcase rather than a finished real-estate photograph.**

## Work orders for r5

- Introduce a real CC0 sunset HDRI for the visible sky/reflection environment.
- Introduce real CC0 PBR maps for facade/plaster and timber.
- Remove the weak procedural palm from the hero frame.
- Replace large single-sphere shrubs with smaller irregular clustered planting masses.
- Reduce exposure and re-balance water so the pool preserves color and reflections.
- Pull the camera back slightly and preserve the complete architectural silhouette.
- Pack third-party images into the generated `.blend` and embed them into the GLB export.
