# Dubai Luxury Villa v0.2 — Visual Critique 03

## Reviewed artifact

`renders/villa-v0.2-hero.png` from critic iteration r3 (Cycles CPU).

## Verdict

**WOW Gate: FAIL**

The scene has improved geometry, a lower camera and more coherent architecture, but the current image still reads as a dark CG/procedural render rather than premium architectural visualization.

## Five highest-impact visible defects

1. **Overall exposure is too low.** The residence loses facade hierarchy and large portions of the frame collapse into black.
2. **The sky/background is not functioning as a believable blue-hour environment.** The frame lacks a readable horizon and atmospheric separation.
3. **Cycles CPU output is visibly noisy in the available GitHub runner build.** This damages material quality, glazing and perceived finish.
4. **Pool water is too dark and reflective detail is weak.** The pool does not act as a premium compositional feature.
5. **The camera still feels slightly elevated / model-like.** The next frame should feel closer to an architectural photograph taken from human eye level.

## Work orders for r4

- Switch the hero review render back to Eevee for a clean presentation pass.
- Use a Nishita sky with restrained low-sun blue-hour lighting.
- Raise exposure and cool ambient fill while preserving warm interiors.
- Rebalance glass and pool materials for readable reflections instead of black transmission.
- Lower the camera and use a photographic 50–55 mm perspective.
- Add a continuous site/context ground so the property does not float against an empty background.
- Keep the current architecture; do not add scope until presentation quality improves.

## Status

`R3_REJECTED_FOR_PRESENTATION`

The r3 render remains useful as engineering evidence, but it is not approved for a portfolio hero image.
