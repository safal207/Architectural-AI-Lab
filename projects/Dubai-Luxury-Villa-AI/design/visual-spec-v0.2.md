# Dubai Luxury Villa v0.2 — Visual Specification

## Goal

Create one portfolio-grade exterior hero asset before expanding scope.

The target is not “technically correct 3D”. The target is a premium architectural image that could plausibly appear in a luxury developer presentation.

## Hero composition

Primary camera:
- three-quarter exterior view
- eye level slightly elevated above pool deck
- camera sees both main facade and pool terrace
- avoid extreme wide-angle distortion
- architecture occupies the majority of the frame
- skyline is optional and must remain secondary

Time of day:
- blue-hour / warm sunset transition
- exterior sky remains readable
- warm interior light creates contrast
- avoid over-dark cinematic grading

## Massing

Use 2 floors only for the hero phase.

Required characteristics:
- strong horizontal composition
- at least one meaningful cantilever
- upper volume appears lighter than the base
- ground floor visually opens toward pool/garden
- street/arrival side remains more private
- no random stacked-box look

## Facade depth

The facade must include real depth cues:
- recessed glazing
- projecting slabs
- framed openings
- deep reveals
- shadow lines
- a defined entry threshold

Flat planes with textures applied are not acceptable.

## Material hierarchy

Primary: warm limestone / travertine
Secondary: natural timber
Structure/accent: dark bronze or charcoal metal
Transparent layer: neutral glass with controlled reflectivity
Water: clean blue-green, not neon cyan
Landscape: dry-climate green, restrained and architectural

Rules:
- maximum 4 dominant exterior materials
- stone carries mass
- timber provides warmth
- metal defines precision
- glass provides openness

## Glazing

Glass must read as glass through:
- believable reflection
- slight tint only
- interior visibility where appropriate
- dark slim frames
- no flat blue translucent panels

## Pool + terrace

The pool is part of the architecture.

Requirements:
- aligned with the main living axis
- strong relationship to facade
- generous pool deck
- optional submerged lounge / shallow shelf
- controlled reflections
- no resort clutter

## Landscape

Use landscape to frame architecture, not hide it.

Required:
- low hedges / grasses
- a few sculptural trees
- palms only where composition benefits
- integrated low-level lighting
- no random tropical jungle

## Lighting

Exterior lighting:
- warm 2700–3000K feel
- recessed soffit lights
- low landscape lights
- subtle wall washing

Interior lighting:
- warm and layered
- enough to reveal life inside
- no blown-out windows

## Interior visibility in hero shot

Show only what supports the exterior:
- living lounge
- dining zone
- warm ceiling or wall finishes

Do not spend hero-phase time detailing every room.

## Hero-view acceptance test

PASS only if:
- silhouette reads clearly at thumbnail size
- no obvious primitive/blockout feeling remains
- massing has hierarchy
- facade has real depth
- materials separate clearly
- glazing is believable
- pool strengthens composition
- landscaping frames rather than distracts
- lighting feels intentional
- image has one dominant focal hierarchy
- first impression is “premium residence”, not “AI demo”

## Secondary views after hero PASS

1. Pool terrace
2. Grand living room
3. Master suite

No additional rooms before these three are convincing.
