# Dubai Luxury Villa v0.2 — Reference Pack

## Purpose

This reference pack is not a style-copy brief. It extracts transferable design principles from high-end Dubai residential architecture so the v0.2 villa can form its own coherent identity.

## Primary references

### 1. SAOTA — Island Villa, Dubai
Source: https://www.saota.com/project/island-villa-dubai-uae/

Extract:
- strong indoor/outdoor continuity
- shaded terraces and courtyards
- landscape treated as part of the architecture
- openness toward views while preserving privacy

Do not copy:
- exact plan
- exact facade composition
- exact proportions

### 2. SAOTA — ATR Villa, Palm Jumeirah
Source: https://www.saota.com/atr-villa/

Extract:
- intersecting horizontal planes
- a clear arrival axis
- central courtyard logic
- limestone + timber + metal + marble material hierarchy
- private upper accommodation with strong horizon connection

### 3. SAOTA — Lanai Island Villas, Dubai
Source: https://www.saota.com/lanai-island-villas/

Extract:
- deep overhangs for shade
- planted upper-level edges
- layered privacy
- perforated/screened facades where useful
- passive climate response without losing openness

### 4. SAOTA — Six Senses Residences Dubai
Source: https://www.saota.com/project/six-senses-residences-dubai/

Extract:
- architecture and landscape as one composition
- shaded outdoor spaces
- paths, water and planting used to create a sense of journey
- privacy without visual heaviness

### 5. Dubai Hills Villa — ALGEDRA
Source: https://algedra.ae/en/architectural-designs/projects/contemporary-luxury-villa-architecture-design-in-dubai

Extract:
- beige travertine / natural stone
- warm timber accents
- large glass surfaces
- ceremonial double-height entrance
- restrained neutral palette

### 6. Villa H — ARK-architecture / ArchDaily
Source: https://www.archdaily.com/1036273/villa-h-ark-architecture

Extract:
- pool as architectural element, not decoration
- sandstone continuity between terrace and pool edge
- quiet street-facing privacy
- central patio / double-height void
- minimal but intentional entry gesture

## Supporting visual cues

Use only as secondary cues, never as the main identity:

- warm sunset exterior lighting
- dark bronze / charcoal window frames
- floor-to-ceiling glazing
- deep roof cantilevers
- reflecting pool / infinity edge
- low layered planting
- palms used sparingly
- warm integrated architectural lighting
- water reflections supporting the hero composition

## Reference hierarchy

1. Architecture and proportion
2. Indoor/outdoor relationship
3. Climate response
4. Material hierarchy
5. Pool + landscape composition
6. Lighting
7. Decorative detail

If a decorative detail conflicts with the architecture, remove the detail.

## Anti-generic rules

Reject any iteration that looks like:
- stacked boxes with glass inserted between them
- a real-estate CGI template
- a generic white villa with palm trees
- random luxury materials without hierarchy
- a pool placed as an afterthought
- excessive LED-strip decoration
- ornamental clutter

## Identity direction for our villa

The v0.2 villa should feel:

- calm, precise and expensive
- warm rather than sterile
- horizontal rather than tower-like
- deeply shaded
- visually open to the pool and horizon
- private from the arrival side
- materially restrained
- believable enough to read as architecture, not an AI demo

## Target phrase

> A climate-aware private Dubai residence where stone, timber, glass, water and shade form one continuous spatial composition.
