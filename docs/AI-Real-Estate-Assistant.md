# AI Real Estate Assistant

## Purpose

Natural language interface for exploring a digital twin.

## Examples

Question:
"What is the area of the master bedroom?"

Answer:
"Master Bedroom — 52 sqm, second floor, terrace access."

## Possible capabilities

- room information
- material descriptions
- navigation hints
- design explanations
- property presentation support

## Principle

AI assists exploration and presentation. Architectural decisions require human review.
